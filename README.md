# Upworthy Headlines: Does Centering a Woman Change Who Clicks?

> Project 2 of my analytics portfolio. In progress.

A Python analysis of thousands of real headline A/B tests run by Upworthy in 2013–2015: what makes a headline get clicked, and whether headlines that center a woman perform differently.

## Data source
The Upworthy Research Archive (Matias, Munger, Aubin Le Quere & Ebersole, Cornell University), on the Open Science Framework: https://osf.io/jd64p/ · CC BY 4.0.
Paper: Matias et al. (2021), "The Upworthy Research Archive, a time series of 32,487 experiments in U.S. media", *Scientific Data* 8, 195. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-00934-7

Download the archive from OSF and unzip it into `data/raw/osf_jd64p/` (the raw files are not committed). `docs/raw_checksums.txt` lists the SHA-256 of every file used.
